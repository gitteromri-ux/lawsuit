Pace in numbers

History depth

Credit accounting

Proven against alleged

229 cases are marked proven, meaning both the commitment and its contradiction are quoted from the same transcript. 219 are marked alleged, meaning the client asserted the failure and the transcript carries no confirming admission. Machine verified quotes
